# Commercial Use and Consultancy

This project, *Cor del Món* (the working repo name is `cor-del-mon`), is a research
and design proposal. It is shared openly so that anyone can read it, learn from it,
cite it, and dream alongside it.

The licenses (`LICENSE` for code, `LICENSE-content.md` for prose and design content)
reserve **commercial use**. This is intentional. The work is offered to the world as
an idea, not as a free shovel for someone else's quarry.

## Who this is for

If you are one of these people, please reach out.

- A **city, regional government, or national ministry** considering Cor del Món
  (or any of its city expressions: Andorra la Vella, Yokohama, Adelaide, Valparaíso,
  Cape Town, Jaipur, or a future Wave-2 city) as a real urban policy and architecture
  initiative.
- A **developer, real-estate group, or design-build contractor** wanting to bring a
  Cor monument or a Llars del Cor housing block from concept to ground-breaking.
- A **publisher, broadcaster, or production studio** seeking to commercially license
  the prose, the visual identity, the architectural language, the soundtrack
  references, or the network narrative for a book, documentary, exhibition, or series.
- A **foundation, philanthropic body, or cultural institution** wanting to fund or
  host a Cor Biennale, a Cor Residency, or a Wave-2 admission process.
- A **brand or sponsor** considering an aligned partnership.

## What is offered

- **Commercial licensing** of any portion of the code, content, or visual identity.
- **Design consultancy**: helping shape your city's Cor expression, choosing the
  monument site, adapting national style and seismic engineering, drafting your
  city's Cor Charter, and planning the milestones for 2030, 2035, 2040, and 2050.
- **Network onboarding**: assistance becoming a Wave-2 member city of Cor del Món
  from 2035 onward.
- **Advisory**: speaking, workshops, masterclasses, or a sustained advisory role.

## How to reach out

Two paths:

- **Public conversation** (preferred for most inquiries): open a structured
  contact thread at
  [github.com/sara-star-quant/cor-del-mon/issues/new?template=contact.yml](https://github.com/sara-star-quant/cor-del-mon/issues/new?template=contact.yml)
  and pick "Commercial inquiry" as the kind.
- **Private email**: **cor-del-mon [at] protonmail [dot] com** for cases that need
  to stay private (confidential commercial discussions, NDA-bound preliminaries,
  individual privacy concerns).

A short note about your role, your city or organisation, and what you have in mind
is enough to start a conversation. Replies usually arrive within a week.

## A note on tone

This is a love project. Conversations about it are welcome from anyone who reads
the work and feels the pulse of it. If you are unsure whether your use is
commercial or not, just ask. The answer is almost always warm.
